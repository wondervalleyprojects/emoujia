# ADR-001: The reading model moves from Gemini's free tier to paid Claude Sonnet 5.5

RECORD · LAB · EMJ-001 · 21 NOV 2026 · JG + MACHINE ASSIST · PROPOSED

**Status:** Decided by Japhy Grant, 21 Nov 2026. Built on the 0.3.0 branch; not live. Written under FND-005 Articles 2.4 and 9.1, and its rule that no metered service starts without the founder's approval and a record of cost, ceiling and shutdown.

**Problem.** Emoujia sent each screenshot to Google's Gemini on the free tier. Google's free terms let it use what is sent to improve its models and let human reviewers read it. That was a logged exception (EMJ-001, 1 Nov 2026) to the studio's rule that what participants share is never offered for training. Google also retires `gemini-2.5-flash`, the model in use, on 1 Dec 2026.

**Options considered.**

1. Stay on Gemini's free tier with a newer model. $0. The exception stays, and the free tier may not be offered in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland.
2. Gemini's paid tier. No training. 1.8 cents a reading on 3.6 Flash, 3.5 cents from 1 Jan 2027.
3. Claude Sonnet 5.5, paid. No training. 1.4 cents a reading.
4. Claude with Gemini's free tier as a fallback. Rejected: a screenshot could still reach Google on the free terms.

**Evidence** (`scripts/model-test.ts`, Japhy's two iPhone screenshots, whole and cropped, 20 and 21 Nov 2026):

| Model | Emoji right | Time | Screenshot reading | Three-emoji reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Opus 5.5 | 98% | about 22s | about 4.5 cents | about 2.4 cents |
| Gemini 3.6 Flash | 94% | 20 to 34s | 1.8 cents, 3.5 from 1 Jan | 0.7 cents, 1.5 from 1 Jan |
| Claude Sonnet 5.5 | 93% | about 10s | about 1.4 cents | about 0.7 cents |
| Claude Haiku 5.5 | 87% | about 15s | about 0.2 cents | under 0.1 cents |
| Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite | 82% | 2 to 4s | 0.2 cents | under 0.1 cents |

Two screenshots is a small sample. Costs are from the token counts of those calls and each company's price list on the day.

**Decision.** Claude Sonnet 5.5 (`claude-sonnet-5-5`) and no other model. No Google fallback.

**Why.** The studio's ethics: people keep the right to their own data, and nothing they share is offered for training. Anthropic's Commercial Terms say it may not train on what is sent, and it deletes it within 30 days unless flagged. Sonnet read as accurately as the best practical Gemini model, in a third of the time.

**Owner.** Japhy Grant.

**Cost.** About 1.4 cents a screenshot reading and 0.7 cents a three-emoji reading. Ceiling: $10 every three months, about 700 screenshot readings. Before this: $0. Labor: about a day of machine-assisted work, reviewed by Japhy. No other new service.

**What happens at the ceiling.** Anthropic refuses the request and the page says "The oracle is resting" until the limit resets or credit is bought. Nothing is charged automatically.

**Preview and production.**

- For the deploy preview, Emoujia uses an existing key in Japhy's default Anthropic workspace, which has a $25 monthly limit shared with other things. So the preview's ceiling is the shared $25, not $10 a quarter.
- Before production: Emoujia moves to its own Anthropic workspace with its own monthly spend limit, with a new key there, swapped into Netlify, and the old key deleted. The resting message is confirmed against that workspace's limit. Steps: `docs/OPERATIONS.md`, "Launch day" and "Capping what Claude can cost".

**Risks.**

- The screenshot still leaves the visitor's device for another company. The notice says so.
- Anthropic may look at what is sent, for safety, during the 30 days, and does not delete a single request on demand.
- Its terms open with "Services under these Terms are not for consumer use"; section A.1 allows powering products for one's own end users. [JAPHY'S READING]
- People under 18: Anthropic asks for extra safeguards where minors use a product directly. Emoujia is not aimed at them. [JAPHY]
- A busy day spends the budget and the oracle rests.
- On the shared workspace, other work and Emoujia can use up each other's limit and rate limit.

**Reversibility.** One setting. `READING_MODELS` in Netlify chooses the model; a `gemini-*` name with `GEMINI_API_KEY` set goes back to Google, and the notices would have to change back with it. The Gemini code is kept, unused.

**Review trigger.** 1 May 2027 (the old exception's review date); or a change in Anthropic's terms or prices; or spend passing $10 in a quarter; or accuracy complaints.

**Tests.** `scripts/model-test.ts`; the bad-input and resting checks described in CHANGELOG 0.3.0; the limit test in OPERATIONS step 5, still to do on the real workspace.
